Our Philosophy

The Story Behind Lopperne

At Lopperne – Scandinavian Design, we are driven by a deep passion for the proud Scandinavian design heritage. Our mission is to counteract today’s throwaway culture by breathing new, lasting life into pre-owned architect-designed furniture through meticulous restoration, gentle care, and profound respect for authentic craftsmanship.

A chair by Wegner, Arne Jacobsen, or Børge Mogensen is not merely a utility object — it is a sculpture, an investment, and a cultural heirloom built to be cherished across generations.
